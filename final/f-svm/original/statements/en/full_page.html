Introduction

In machine learning, classification tasks often require algorithms that can accurately distinguish between different categories of data. Support Vector Machines (SVM) are a powerful family of linear classifiers designed for this purpose.

Let’s consider a dataset of n n n points in a two-dimensional space, each belonging to one of two classes labeled as l i = ± 1 l_i=\pm{1} li​=±1. We assume that these points can be perfectly separated by a hyperplane. Among all possible separating hyperplanes, we seek the one that maximizes the margin, which is the distance between the hyperplane and the nearest data points of both classes. This optimal hyperplane is defined by its support vectors.

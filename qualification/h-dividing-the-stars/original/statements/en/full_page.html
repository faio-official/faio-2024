Dividing the Stars

Time limit2 seconds
Memory limit256 Mb
Inputstandard input or input.txt
Outputstandard output or output.txt

Neutron stars and giant stars are marked on a flat map. No three stars lie on one straight line. The number n is given. The next n lines contain the coordinates of the neutron stars (2 real numbers separated by a space), and the following n lines contain the coordinates of the giant stars (2 real numbers separated by a space).

Draw a line y = ax + b that passes through one neutron star and one giant star such that, if you count the stars in one of the half-planes, the number of neutron stars equals the number of giant stars. It is guaranteed that a solution of the form y = ax + b exists.

Output a and b separated by a space.

Constraints: 1 < n < 10^5. The coordinates of a star are real numbers greater than 0 and less than 10^6.

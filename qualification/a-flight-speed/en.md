Arman and his assistants are flying from Earth to the space station to repair the gravitational matrix. On a graph of time (hours since launch) against distance (kilometers from Earth), the path of their rocket is a branch of the parabola y = ax^2 + bx + c. They want to know the exact speed of their rocket d hours after launch.

The real numbers a, b, c and d are given on separate lines. 0 < a, b, c, d < 1000.

Output the answer to the problem.

## Input

```
10
4.65116
7.178
12
```

## Output

```
244.65116
```

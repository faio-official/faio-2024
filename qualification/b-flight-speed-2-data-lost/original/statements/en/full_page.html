Flight Speed 2: Data Lost

Time limit1 second
Memory limit64 Mb
Inputstandard input or input.txt
Outputstandard output or output.txt

A team of space engineers flew to the station, but all the plans from the previous problem were ruined by space debris, which changed their speed at the very start of the journey. It is known that the new time-distance graph is also a parabola. Runi managed to record data about their ship at only three points before the sensor broke too. They hope this is enough to recover the graph.

Find the real numbers a, b, c of the parabola ax^2 + bx + c, given that it passes through three points with real coordinates. The first point has coordinates (x1, y1). The second point lies x2 to the left of the first point and y2 above the third point. The third point has coordinates (x3, y3). The numbers x1, y1, x2, y2, x3, y3 are given separated by spaces. Output the three numbers a, b, c separated by spaces.

All the variables mentioned are greater than -1000 and less than 1000.

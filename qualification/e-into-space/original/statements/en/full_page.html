Into Space

Time limit1 second
Memory limit64 Mb
Inputstandard input or input.txt
Outputstandard output or output.txt

The team needs to place 4 magnets in one plane. For the magnets to work smoothly, the pairwise distances between them must be the natural numbers a, b, c, d, e and f meters. The team is not sure whether this is possible. Help them figure it out.

The first line contains these 6 numbers separated by spaces.

If it is possible, output "yes". If it is impossible, output "no".

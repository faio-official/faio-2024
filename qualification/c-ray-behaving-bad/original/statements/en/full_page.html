Ray is behaving bad

Time limit1 second
Memory limit64 Mb
Inputstandard input or input.txt
Outputstandard output or output.txt

Ray is on the first floor of a space station consisting of a single point. The second and third floors of the station are regular pentagons that have sides but no diagonals. A point on the first floor is connected by stairs to each vertex on the second floor. Each point on the second floor is connected by stairs to the two points closest to it. In addition, each vertex on the third floor is connected to two stairs leading from the second floor. The fourth floor is just a single point, and each vertex on the third floor is connected to a point on the fourth floor by a staircase. Ray happily moves from one point to another, doing this without taking breaks. When he finds himself at a vertex, he randomly and equiprobably chooses one of the available stairs and moves along it to the next vertex in one minute. What is the probability that he will reach the fourth floor in 3 minutes? Print your answer as a decimal fraction (float).
